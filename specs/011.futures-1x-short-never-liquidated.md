# 011 — A 1x futures short is never liquidated

Bug spec (Definition / Evidence / Assumption).

## §1 Definition

A futures DCA leg (also each leg of a hedge) tracks one position per pair
and closes every open deal of the pair when the bar reaches the position's
liquidation price (`checkPosition`).

- **§1.1 Expected:** at leverage 1 a short's margin equals its notional, so
  it is liquidated when the price reaches twice its entry, at about −100%,
  the way the exchange closes it.
- **§1.2 Actual:** the 1x short liquidation price is `entry / userFee`
  (2000× entry at a 0.05% fee). It is never reached, so a losing 1x short
  stays open and can close far past −100% (−188% in the evidence below).
- **§1.3 Unchanged:** the 1x long liquidation price (`entry × userFee`,
  close to zero) and the `1 ± 1/leverage` price for leverage above 1.

## §2 Evidence

- Replay of a saved multi-pair hedge backtest (binanceUsdm, 50 pairs, 1x
  isolated on both legs, leg TP/SL off, hedge TP 3 / SL −9, 1h candles from
  the exchange's public API) through the real engine reproduces the saved
  run exactly: 157 long / 201 short deals, max deal loss −188.42%, max deal
  profit +194.12%. The −188.42% deal is a QUSDT short entered at 0.026094
  and closed at 0.075212; the first bar whose high reaches 2× entry
  (0.052188) is 2026-09-27 11:00Z, where nothing happened.
- `test/futures-1x-short-liquidation.spec.ts` §1.1 fails on the old code
  (the deal is still open after a 150% rise).

## §3 Assumption (root cause)

`updatePositionWithOrder`'s liquidation price uses `1 / userFee` as the
leverage ≤ 1 short multiplier, a placeholder for "never" that has been
there since the engine's first commit. Replacing it with `2` (the
`1 + 1/leverage` form at leverage 1) fixes it.

## §4 Blast radius

- Every futures DCA/combo backtest at leverage 1 with a short leg, server
  side (main-app backtest worker) and client side (dashboards' in-browser
  DCA and hedge backtests). Consumers must take the bump together.
- `marginType: inherit` is modelled as leverage 1 by the `leverage` getter,
  so inherit shorts now liquidate at 2× entry too. Any real leverage ≥ 1
  liquidates at or below 2×, so this is the loosest correct bound.
- Golden fixtures (`hooks-parity`, `single-position` parity, smoothing):
  unchanged, the full suite is green under the recorded timezone.

## §5 Out of scope

- Grid's `getLiquidationPrice` has the same 1x short placeholder; not
  touched here.
- The hedge TP/SL is evaluated on bar closes, so a pair can close past its
  SL when one candle moves through it. That is as built.
