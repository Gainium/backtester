# 015 — Unrealized PnL in USD is valued at the last bar of the test

Bug spec (Definition / Evidence / Assumption).

## §1 Definition

At the end of a DCA/combo backtest the deals still open are valued at the
close of the last bar (`markPrice`). Their unrealized PnL is reported in the
profit currency (`unrealizedPnL`, per-deal `unrealized.total`), in USD
(`unrealizedPnLUsd`, per-deal `unrealized.totalUsd`) and as a % of the capital
they hold (`unrealizedPnLPerc`, per-deal `unrealized.perc`, over
`unrealizedUsage`).

- **§1.1 Expected:** the USD figure is the profit-currency figure converted at
  the USD rate implied by the last bar (the same price the deals are valued
  at), as realized profit is converted at the deal's own price.
- **§1.2 Expected:** none of the unrealized figures depend on the host's live
  price list (`prices`, today's price).
- **§1.3 Not changing:** trades, deals and realized profit are unchanged.
- **Actual:** the USD conversion and the usage denominator used `usdRate` /
  `getRate()`, which are built once in the constructor from the live price
  list. A base-profit BTCUSDT long tested over 2022 showed an unrealized USD
  loss of 64,577 USD per BTC (today's price) instead of 16,614 (last bar).

## §2 Evidence

- A shared prod backtest (spot long, base profit, BTCUSDT, 2022) stores
  `unrealizedPnL -0.409134 BTC`, `unrealizedPnLUsd -26420.85` (= 64,577/BTC)
  and `quoteRate 16613.56` (the last bar). The mirrored short stores
  -0.000039 BTC = -2.54 USD (64,717/BTC). The backtest runs in the browser, so
  no prod log exists; the arithmetic reproduces in the engine.
- `test/unrealized-pnl-usd-at-last-bar.spec.ts` on 1.31.1: with the live
  price at 4× the last close, the LONG USD loss is 4× the base loss × last
  close; every unrealized field changes with the live price.

## §3 Assumption

`getProfitStats` converts `unPnl` with `this.usdRate` (live) and scales the
usage with `getRate()` (live), instead of the rate at `markPrice`.

## §4 Pairs not quoted in USDT

- **§4.1 Expected:** on a pair like BTC/USDC, a base-currency amount (base
  profit, unrealized or realized) is converted at the pair's price at the bar
  times the quote's USD rate, in DCA/combo (`getUsdRate`) and grid
  (`loadData`'s `usdRate`). None of it depends on the live price list.
- **§4.2 Not changing:** USDT-quoted pairs take the exact same code path as
  before (the new branch is gated on `quoteAsset !== 'USDT'`).
- **Actual (1.32.0):** `findUSDRate(BASE)` tries BASE/USDT first, and the
  host's live list carries BTC/USDT at today's price, so the pair's own bar
  price was ignored. When the live list also had no USDC/USDT entry,
  `findUSDRate(USDC)` crossed through the pair itself (USDC→BTC→USDT) and came
  back to the live BTC/USDT price, so the quote rate is read from the live list
  without the pair.
- **Evidence:** `test/unrealized-pnl-usd-non-usdt-quote.spec.ts` and
  `test/grid-usd-rate-non-usdt-quote.spec.ts`: before the fix the USD figures
  scale 4x with a 4x live price (DCA unrealized -2153.85 vs -538.46, realized
  139.59 vs 34.95; grid profit 21.91 vs 5.48); after, they are identical.

## Invariants

None added: library with no persisted state (`unrealized*` is a derived stat).
