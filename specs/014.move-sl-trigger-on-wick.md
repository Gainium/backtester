# 014 — Move SL: the trigger is reached by a wick, as in live trading

Bug spec (Definition / Evidence / Assumption).

## §1 Definition

A DCA/combo bot with **Move SL** (`moveSL`, `moveSLTrigger`, `moveSLValue`)
moves its stop to `moveSLValue` once the price has gone `moveSLTrigger` %
(+ 2× fee) in the deal's favour.

- **§1.1 Expected:** in a backtest the stop moves as soon as any price in the
  bar segment being checked reaches the trigger, so a wick counts. That is how
  live bots behave: the live engine arms the move at
  `avgPrice × (1 ± (trigger + 2·fee))` and fires on the first tick that
  reaches it.
- **§1.2 Expected:** on a long green candle (open → low → high → close), the
  stop moved on the high is checked only on the high → close part of the bar,
  so the deal is not stopped out at entry on a low the price reached *before*
  the trigger. The same holds for the mirrored short red candle.
- **§1.3 Expected:** a wick that does not reach the trigger still moves
  nothing.
- **Actual:** `checkValue` measured the trigger at the segment's **adverse**
  extreme (low for a long, high for a short). The stop moved only when the
  whole segment was beyond the trigger. A candle whose wick hit the first TP
  and the trigger but closed back did not move the stop, and the deal later
  closed at the original stop.

## §2 Evidence

- Reporter's bots (read-only GraphQL): 1D BTC-USDC long and short, Move SL
  trigger 2.9 % → 0.2 %, multi-TP with the first TP at ~3 %, SL by indicator
  (`techInd`). The take-profit check (`filterTP`) uses the favourable extreme,
  so the first TP fills on the wick. The move-SL check used the adverse one.
  This matches "the first TP was hit on a wick but the SL did not move".
- Replay through the real engine (`test/move-sl-trigger-wick.spec.ts`) on
  1.28.1: a long opened at 100 sees a bar with high 101.5 (trigger 1 %
  + 0.1 % fee = 101.1) and low 99.9. The stop is not moved, and the deal closes
  at the original -5 % stop (95.09). Short mirror: 104.9. Same on the green
  candle. The `techInd` stop does the same.
- No prod log exists for this path: the backtest runs in the browser (V2) and
  in main-app's backtest worker, which does not log per-bar decisions.

## §3 Assumption (root cause)

`fa005af` ("movesl fromula", 2024-03-13) replaced the unrealised-PnL formula
with a price-distance formula. In doing so it swapped the price it reads from
`b.high`/`b.low` (long/short, favourable) to `b.low`/`b.high` (adverse).
The callers already pass the sub-bar on which the favourable move happens
(`bOpenHigh`/`bOpenLow`, or the full bar after the adverse leg was checked),
and then check the moved stop only on the remaining segment
(`bHighClose`/`bLowClose`). So reading the favourable extreme is consistent
with the bar-path model.

## §4 Blast radius

- `src/dca/strategy/main.ts` `checkValue` only. It is shared by DCA and combo
  backtests (combo goes through the same `Strategy`; `comboBotFunctions` has
  no trigger logic). Grid and hedge are not affected, other than hedge legs
  that are DCA strategies.
- Take-profit, trailing and live trading are unchanged.
- Danger List #13: server-side (main-app) and client-side (main-dash-redesign)
  results change identically once both pin this version.
